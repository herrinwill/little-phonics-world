# Letter sound credits
- English letter sounds (audio/sound/en, 26 clips): Open Letter Sounds by Purple Computer, https://github.com/purplecomputerorg/openlettersounds — CC0 1.0 (public domain). Female voice.
- Spanish, Hebrew, Greek and Thai letter sounds (audio/sound/he|el|th|es): Microsoft Edge neural voices, all female — es-MX-DaliaNeural, he-IL-HilaNeural, el-GR-AthinaNeural, th-TH-PremwadeeNeural (plus es-US-PalomaNeural for Spanish J). Where a voice could not say a sound on its own, the same sound is borrowed from another female voice: Hebrew ו and פּ use Athina ("va", "pa"); Greek Α and Ε use Dalia ("a", "e"); Greek Ι and Υ reuse Athina's Η ("ee").
- All other game speech (English words, names, explanations, Buddy cheers): Microsoft Edge neural voice en-US-AvaNeural (female); native words use the same female voices as above.
